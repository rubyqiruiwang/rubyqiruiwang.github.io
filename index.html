Research

Working papers & work in progress
In progress

Do Leaders Export Pollution While Importing Wealth? Evidence of “Green Favoritism” in Autocratic Political Economies

with Richard Schulz and Muhammad Majid Altaf · advised by Joshua Blumenstock

Tests whether political leaders steer economic activity toward their birth regions while pushing pollution elsewhere. Builds an ADM2 district-year panel from DMSP and VIIRS nighttime lights and TROPOMI NO₂ / PM₂.₅ satellite data, extracted through Google Earth Engine pipelines I wrote.

2025 · under revision

Environmental Regulation and Left-behind Children

Solo-authored

How the intensity of environmental regulation in China affects migrant and left-behind children, using China Migrants Dynamic Survey data and a two-way fixed-effects design. Being reworked for submission to an English-language journal.

In progress

National AI Policy through the Sustainable Shared-Prosperity Policy Index

IRLE, UC Berkeley · with Prof. Clair Brown

Applies the SSPI framework to national AI policy by constructing hand-coded AI policy indicators in the index's style, to compare how countries govern AI and who shares in its gains.

In progress

Who Cares for China’s Elderly? Family Caregivers and Access to Elder Care

Solo-authored

Uses CHARLS data to examine how children, children-in-law and spouses share the work of caring for older adults in China.

Policy essay

Agriculture over Farmers: Grain Policy and Agricultural Labor in Henan Province

Solo-authored

Argues, through a 农 (agriculture) versus 民 (farmers) framework, that China’s sannong agenda treats grain as a strategic resource ahead of farmer welfare.
